🌴 21-Day Bali Master Itinerary: Day-by-Day Route

This 3-week framework uses a counter-clockwise route to avoid Bali's heavy traffic. You'll spend 4 days in Uluwatu, 7 days in Ubud & Highlands, 4 days on the Nusa Islands, and 6 days in Canggu/Seminyak.

Phase 1: Uluwatu & Bukit Peninsula (Days 1–4)

Base: Uluwatu / Bingin

Day 1: Arrival & Coastal Welcome

  • Morning/Afternoon: Arrive at Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS). Use the e-VOA Autogates to bypass entry lines, grab a Telkomsel eSIM, and take a pre-booked transfer south to Uluwatu (45–60 mins).
  • Late Afternoon: Check into your villa or hotel and relax after your flight.
  • Evening: Head down to Thomas Beach for fresh grilled fish at a beachfront warung while watching the sunset.

Day 2: Cliffline Beaches & Kecak Fire Dance

  • Morning: Visit Padang Padang Beach early (8:00 AM) to beat the crowds and enjoy calm morning swimming conditions.
  • Afternoon: Have lunch in Bingin Village, then spend the afternoon resting along the limestone cove at Bingin Beach.
  • Evening: Arrive at Uluwatu Temple by 4:30 PM to explore the cliff tops before watching the iconic 6:00 PM Kecak Fire Dance at sunset.

Day 3: Secret Coves & Sunset Lounges

  • Morning: Head to Green Bowl Beach early (note the ~300 steep steps down and back up) or visit the wide sands of Melasti Beach.
  • Afternoon: Grab lunch at Single Fin or El Kabron overlooking the surf breaks.
  • Evening: Enjoy a relaxed dinner in Single Fin / Suluban Beach area.

Day 4: Balangan Lookout & Transition Prep

  • Morning: Visit Balangan Beach and walk up to the cliff viewpoint for panoramic views of the coast.
  • Afternoon: Relax at a cafe in Anantara / Padang Padang, pack up, and prepare for your transfer inland tomorrow.
  • Evening: Casual dinner at Warung Local or Cashew Tree in Bingin.

Phase 2: Cultural Heart & Highlands – Ubud (Days 5–11)

Base: Ubud Central / Penestanan

Day 5: Transit North & Sacred Monkey Forest

  • Morning: Check out of Uluwatu and take a private transfer north to Ubud (1.5 to 2 hours).
  • Afternoon: Check in and visit the Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary to walk through moss-covered temples and ancient banyan trees.
  • Evening: Stroll the Campuhan Ridge Walk at golden hour (5:30 PM), followed by dinner at Warung Babi Guling Ibu Oka.

Day 6: Subak Terraces & Holy Water Purification

  • Morning: Arrive at Tegallalang Rice Terraces by 7:30 AM to explore the UNESCO Subak irrigation trails before tour buses arrive.
  • Afternoon: Drive 20 minutes north to Tirta Empul Temple for a traditional Melukat purification ritual, then visit the nearby 11th-century Gunung Kawi cliff shrines.
  • Evening: Traditional Balinese dinner at Locavore Noodle or Warung Makan Bu Rus.

Day 7: Hidden Waterfalls of Gianyar

  • Morning: Visit Tukad Cepung Waterfall between 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM to capture light rays beaming through the cave ceiling.
  • Afternoon: Stop by Tibumana Waterfall for a swim in its natural freshwater pool, followed by lunch overlooking local rice fields.
  • Evening: Attend a traditional Legong Dance Performance at the Ubud Palace (7:30 PM).

Day 8: Mount Batur Volcanic Sunrise Trek

  • Early Morning: 2:30 AM pick-up for the guided hike up Mount Batur (1,717m). Reach the crater summit by 6:00 AM for breakfast at sunrise above the clouds.
  • Late Morning: Descend the volcano and soak in the Batur Natural Hot Springs along Lake Batur to rest your muscles.
  • Afternoon: Return to Ubud by 2:00 PM for a well-deserved nap and massage.
  • Evening: Dinner at Sayan House overlooking the Ayung River valley.

Day 9: Organic Cooking Class & Craft Villages

  • Morning: Take an organic farm-to-table cooking class starting with an early tour of the Ubud Local Market.
  • Afternoon: Visit local artisan villages: Mas (woodcarving), Celuk (silversmithing), and Penglipuran Traditional Village.
  • Evening: Relaxed evening with sound healing at the Pyramids of Chi or dinner in Nyuh Kuning.

Day 10: UNESCO Jatiluwih Terraces & Bedugul

  • Morning: Day trip northwest to the vast, breathtaking Jatiluwih Rice Terraces for a 2-hour walk through the rice fields.
  • Afternoon: Visit Pura Ulun Danu Bratan, the famous floating temple on Lake Bratan in Bedugul.
  • Evening: Return to Ubud for your final night in the central highlands.

Day 11: Munduk Waterfalls Excursion

  • Morning: Explore northern Munduk's lush green trails, visiting Banyu Wana Amertha or Red Coral Waterfall.
  • Afternoon: Return toward central Bali, packing and preparing for tomorrow's island-hopping fast boat.
  • Evening: Dinner at Clear Cafe or Melting Wok Warung in Ubud.

Phase 3: Offshore Paradise – Nusa Islands (Days 12–15)

Base: Nusa Penida / Nusa Lembongan

Day 12: Fast Boat to Nusa Penida (West Coast)

  • Morning: Transfer from Ubud to Sanur Port (45 mins) and take an 8:30 AM fast boat to Nusa Penida (45 mins).
  • Afternoon: Hire a local driver to visit the west coast highlights: Kelingking Beach (the famous T-Rex cliff), Broken Beach, and Angel’s Billabong.
  • Evening: Check in to your Penida hotel and enjoy a quiet sunset dinner at Penida Colada.

Day 13: Manta Ray Snorkeling & East Penida

  • Morning: Take a 7:30 AM boat excursion to Manta Point and Crystal Bay to swim alongside giant manta rays.
  • Afternoon: Drive east to explore Diamond Beach, Atuh Beach, and the Molenteng Treehouse viewpoint.
  • Evening: Dinner on the beach at Amok Sunset.

Day 14: Nusa Lembongan & Yellow Bridge

  • Morning: Take a short 15-minute local boat crossing to Nusa Lembongan.
  • Afternoon: Rent a scooter or shuttle, cross the iconic Yellow Bridge to Nusa Ceningan, and visit the Blue Lagoon.
  • Evening: Watch the crashing waves at Devil’s Tear on Lembongan before sunset drinks at Ohana's.

Day 15: Mangrove Forest & Return to Main Island

  • Morning: Take a peaceful morning wooden boat tour through the Nusa Lembongan Mangrove Forest.
  • Afternoon: Catch a 2:00 PM fast boat back to Sanur Port on the main island.
  • Evening: Private transfer to Canggu (1 to 1.5 hours depending on traffic) and check into your villa.

Phase 4: West Coast Culture, Surf & Culinary – Canggu & Seminyak (Days 16–21)

Base: Canggu / Berawa

Day 16: Surf, Cafes & Echo Beach

  • Morning: Take a morning surf lesson at Batu Bolong Beach or Berawa Beach.
  • Afternoon: Walk through local boutiques along Jalan Batu Bolong and enjoy a healthy bowl at Crate Cafe or Nalu Bowls.
  • Evening: Sunset drinks at La Brisa or Echo Beach Club with dinner right by the water.

Day 17: Boutique Shopping & Fine Dining in Seminyak

  • Morning: Spend the morning exploring the boutiques, galleries, and home decor shops along Jalan Kayu Aya in Seminyak.
  • Afternoon: Relax with a spa treatment at Bodyworks or Potato Head Beach Club.
  • Evening: Reserve a table for fine dining at Mama San, Bambu, or Merah Putih.

Day 18: Tanah Lot Sea Temple

  • Morning: Early breakfast at Milk & Madu followed by a relaxing morning by your villa pool.
  • Afternoon: Drive 45 minutes north to Tanah Lot Temple, set on an offshore rock outcrop. Explore the surrounding grounds.
  • Evening: Enjoy sunset at Tanah Lot as the tide rises, then return to Canggu for dinner.

Day 19: Day Trip to Uluwatu / Beach Club Experience

  • Morning: Optional day trip back down to Melasti Beach or a day pass at Sunday's Beach Club.
  • Afternoon: Relax on the sunbeds, swim, and kayak along the quiet cove.
  • Evening: Return to Canggu for night markets at Samadi Sunday Market or dinner at Mason.

Day 20: Culinary Feast & Farewell Sunset

  • Morning: Casual cafe hopping in Pererenan (quieter area right next to Canggu).
  • Afternoon: Souvenir shopping at Love Anchor Market for handmade jewelry, sarongs, and crafts.
  • Evening: Farewell dinner at Ji Restaurant (Japanese-fusion with sunset ocean views) or FINNS Beach Club.

Day 21: Final Spa & Departure

  • Morning: Enjoy a relaxed final breakfast, a 1-hour Balinese massage, and finish your packing.
  • Afternoon: Transfer from Canggu to Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) (1 to 1.5 hours; head to the airport 3.5 hours before international departures).
