## 🚀 Step-by-Step Application Guide

1. **Academic Evaluation & Direct University Application**
   * Apply through individual university admissions portals (e.g., Polimi Online Services, Unibo Studenti Online) during early call windows (typically September to February for international applicants).
   * Upload transcripts, course descriptions (syllabus), CV (Europass format recommended), Statement of Purpose (SOP), and Recommendation Letters.

2. **Pre-Enrolment via Universitaly**
   * Once you receive an offer or pre-admission letter, register on the official Italian government portal **Universitaly**.
   * Fill out the pre-enrolment application and select the university and course. The university validates this document directly.

3. **Document Legalization & Declaration of Value (DoV) / CIMEA**
   * Obtain a **Statement of Comparability and Verification** from CIMEA or apply for a **Declaration of Value** (*Dichiarazione di Valore*) from the Italian Embassy/Consulate in your home country.

4. **Visa Application (National Type D - Study)**
   * Submit the validated Universitaly pre-enrolment summary alongside financial proof (~€6,000–€7,000 per year), accommodation proof, health insurance, and flight reservations to the consulate.

5. **Arrival in Italy & Post-Arrival Formalities**
   * **Permesso di Soggiorno (Residence Permit):** Apply within 8 working days of arrival in Italy via the postal kit (*Kit Giallo*).
   * **Codice Fiscale:** Obtain your Italian tax code for housing contracts and bank accounts.
   * **Final Matriculation:** Present original physical certificates to the university registrar to complete enrolment.
