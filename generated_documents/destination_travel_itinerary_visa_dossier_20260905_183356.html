🛂 Step-by-Step Visa & Entry Checklist for Bali

1. Apply for the Official 30-Day e-VoA (B1 Tourist Visa)

  • Official Website: Use strictly evisa.imigrasi.go.id (Avoid third-party agency sites charging marked-up processing fees).
  • Cost: IDR 500,000 (~$32 USD / ₹2,700).
  • Validity: Valid for 30 days upon entry; extendable online once for an additional 30 days.
  • Requirements:
  • Passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your arrival date (with at least 2 blank pages).
  • High-resolution scan of your passport bio-page (JPEG/PNG).
  • Formal passport-style photo with a white background.
  • Confirmed outbound/return flight ticket departing Indonesia within 30 days.
  • Pro Tip: Completing the e-VoA online allows you to use the automated biometrics eGates at DPS Airport, reducing immigration queue time to under 2 minutes.

2. Complete the All Indonesia Arrival Card (AIAC) / Customs Declaration

  • Official Website: allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id
  • Cost: FREE
  • Window: Fill this out online within 72 hours prior to your flight.
  • Output: Generates a QR code on your phone to scan at customs before exiting the terminal.

3. Pay the Bali Provincial Tourist Levy

  • Official Website: lovebali.baliprov.go.id
  • Cost: IDR 150,000 (~$10 USD / ₹810) per person.
  • Execution: Pay online prior to departure and keep the digital QR voucher saved on your phone.
