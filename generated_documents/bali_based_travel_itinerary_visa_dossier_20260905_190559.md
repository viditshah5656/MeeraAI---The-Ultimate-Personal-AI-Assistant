### Day 2: Cliffline Beaches & Kecak Fire Dance
* **Morning:** Visit **Padang Padang Beach** early (**8:00 AM**) to beat the crowds and enjoy calm morning swimming conditions.
* **Afternoon:** Have lunch in **Bingin Village**, then spend the afternoon resting along the limestone cove at **Bingin Beach**.

## Phase 2: Cultural Heart & Highlands – Ubud (Days 5–11)
*Base: Ubud Central / Penestanan*

### Day 5: Transit North & Sacred Monkey Forest
* **Morning:** Check out of Uluwatu and take a private transfer north to **Ubud** (**1.5 to 2 hours**).
* **Afternoon:** Check in and visit the **Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary** to walk through moss-covered temples and ancient banyan trees.
* **Evening:** Stroll the **Campuhan Ridge Walk** at golden hour (**5:30 PM**), followed by dinner at **Warung Babi Guling Ibu Oka**.

### Day 6: Subak Terraces & Holy Water Purification
* **Morning:** Arrive at **Tegallalang Rice Terraces** by **7:30 AM** to explore the UNESCO Subak irrigation trails before tour buses arrive.
* **Afternoon:** Drive 20 minutes north to **Tirta Empul Temple** for a traditional **Melukat** purification ritual, then visit the nearby 11th-century **Gunung Kawi** cliff shrines.
* **Evening:** Traditional Balinese dinner at **Locavore Noodle** or **Warung Makan Bu Rus**.

### Day 7: Hidden Waterfalls of Gianyar
* **Morning:** Visit **Tukad Cepung Waterfall** between **9:00 AM and 11:00 AM** to capture light rays beaming through the cave ceiling.
* **Afternoon:** Stop by **Tibumana Waterfall** for a swim in its natural freshwater pool, followed by lunch overlooking local rice fields.
* **Evening:** Attend a traditional **Legong Dance Performance** at the **Ubud Palace** (**7:30 PM**).

### Day 8: Mount Batur Volcanic Sunrise Trek
* **Early Morning:** **2:30 AM** pick-up for the guided hike up **Mount Batur** (**1,717m**). Reach the crater summit by **6:00 AM** for breakfast at sunrise above the clouds.
* **Late Morning:** Descend the volcano and soak in the **Batur Natural Hot Springs** along Lake Batur to rest your muscles.
* **Afternoon:** Return to Ubud by **2:00 PM** for a well-deserved nap and massage.
* **Evening:** Dinner at **Sayan House** overlooking the Ayung River valley.

### Day 9: Organic Cooking Class & Craft Villages
* **Morning:** Take an organic farm-to-table cooking class starting with an early tour of the **Ubud Local Market**.
* **Afternoon:** Visit local artisan villages: **Mas** (woodcarving), **Celuk** (silversmithing), and **Penglipuran Traditional Village**.
* **Evening:** Relaxed evening with sound healing at the **Pyramids of Chi** or dinner in **Nyuh Kuning**.

### Day 10: UNESCO Jatiluwih Terraces & Bedugul
* **Morning:** Day trip northwest to the vast, breathtaking **Jatiluwih Rice Terraces** for a 2-hour walk through the rice fields.
* **Afternoon:** Visit **Pura Ulun Danu Bratan**, the famous floating temple on Lake Bratan in Bedugul.
* **Evening:** Return to Ubud for your final night in the central highlands.

### Day 11: Munduk Waterfalls Excursion
* **Morning:** Explore northern Munduk's lush green trails, visiting **Banyu Wana Amertha** or **Red Coral Waterfall**.
* **Afternoon:** Return toward central Bali, packing and preparing for tomorrow's island-hopping fast boat.
* **Evening:** Dinner at **Clear Cafe** or **Melting Wok Warung** in Ubud.

### Day 13: Manta Ray Snorkeling & East Penida
* **Morning:** Take a **7:30 AM** boat excursion to **Manta Point** and **Crystal Bay** to swim alongside giant manta rays.
* **Afternoon:** Drive east to explore **Diamond Beach**, **Atuh Beach**, and the **Molenteng Treehouse viewpoint**.
* **Evening:** Dinner on the beach at **Amok Sunset**.

### Day 14: Nusa Lembongan & Yellow Bridge
* **Morning:** Take a short **15-minute** local boat crossing to **Nusa Lembongan**.
* **Afternoon:** Rent a scooter or shuttle, cross the iconic **Yellow Bridge** to **Nusa Ceningan**, and visit the **Blue Lagoon**.
* **Evening:** Watch the crashing waves at **Devil’s Tear** on Lembongan before sunset drinks at **Ohana's**.

### Day 15: Mangrove Forest & Return to Main Island
* **Morning:** Take a peaceful morning wooden boat tour through the **Nusa Lembongan Mangrove Forest**.
* **Afternoon:** Catch a **2:00 PM** fast boat back to **Sanur Port** on the main island.
